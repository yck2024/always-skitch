# 0013 Skitch canvas has a margin around the Background, and annotations stay partly on the image

On the single-image page (Skitch) the Fabric canvas is now the **Background** plus a 48 screen-px **margin** on every side, and a moved or resized **Annotation** is clamped so at least 20 screen px of it stays over the image. The exported PNG is unchanged: it is cropped to exactly the Background's size and content, so the margin and anything hanging over the image edge never reach it. Freeform is not affected (it has its own canvas model, ADR-0002).

Before this, the canvas element was exactly the image size. Fabric cannot draw or hit-test controls outside its element, so the resize and rotate handles of an annotation at the image edge were cut off (a rectangle drawn past the corner kept only its top-left handle), nothing stopped an annotation being dragged fully off the image, and a **Step** dragged off the edge kept driving the next number (ADR-0009 assumed every Step stays visible) while nothing on screen could select it again except Undo.

## Considered options

- **Margin only, no clamp**: rejected. The margin only helps handles of annotations that are still mostly on the image; an annotation can still be dragged out past the margin and lost.
- **Clamp only, no margin**: rejected. Handles of an annotation flush with an edge would still be half cut off, because Fabric cannot draw them outside the element.
- **Clamp so the whole annotation stays inside the image**: rejected. Annotations legitimately overhang the edge (a callout near a corner, a box that continues off the screenshot); the export just crops them. Requiring "at least partly on the image" keeps that freedom while guaranteeing there is always something to grab.
- **Delete annotations that end up off the image**: rejected. Deleting is destructive and surprising; clamping loses nothing, and Undo still covers mistakes.
- **A 24 px margin (the issue's first suggestion)**: rejected in favour of 48. Fabric's rotate knob sits 40 px above the top edge of the box plus its radius, so with 24 px the knob of an annotation flush with the top edge would still be clipped. 48 keeps every handle, including that knob, fully visible. The margin is a screen size, so it does not grow or shrink with the fit-to-window zoom.
- **Leave Step numbering as `max + 1` over all Steps, or skip Steps that are off the image**: kept `max + 1`. With the clamp a Step can no longer end up fully off the image, and history is in-memory only, so no old state can hold one; ADR-0009's assumption is now enforced rather than hoped for, and no special case is needed.

## Consequences

- `fitCanvasToViewport` (`src/components/CanvasEditor.tsx`) fits the Background into the window minus the margin, sizes the element as image plus margin, and sets the viewport transform to zoom plus a pan of the margin (Fabric's `getScenePoint`, hit-testing and handle drawing already follow the viewport transform, so scene coordinates and the sticky-tool hit test of ADR-0012 are unchanged). A slightly smaller fit scale is the only visible cost: a screenshot that used to fill the window now leaves 48 px on each side.
- Export crops with `toDataURL({ left, top, width, height, multiplier })` to the Background. The crop is half a source pixel larger than the image because Fabric truncates the output size and `width * scale * (1 / scale)` can land just under the integer; without it a 2400 px image exported as 2399 px once the crop was introduced. The exported size is now exact for any scale.
- The clamp runs on `object:moving` (live, so the annotation visibly stops) and again on `object:modified`, before history is saved, so a resize that dragged the box off is pulled back and Undo restores the clamped state. For a multi-selection every member is kept over the image individually; if the selection is wider than the image the position is left alone.
- New annotations are anchored on the image: drawing pointers (Rectangle, Arrow, Blur) and click placements (Text, Step) are clamped to the Background. If a drawing or click starts in the margin, it is placed at the nearest point on the image edge; a drag that overshoots the edge likewise stops there. This is necessary because the 48 px margin would otherwise let Text or Step clicks, or Rectangle starts, create annotations entirely off the image and lost. Text is then shifted inward as needed so at least 20 screen px remains over the Background. The sticky-tool "did I click an annotation" test still uses the raw pointer.
- The margin is painted a light grey so the screenshot reads as an inset and handles sit on a visible surface.
- The README's description of edge behaviour is updated; the Shortcuts modal needs no change.
